# Retail 12.1 acceptance

Run against the exact review ZIP/commit after `/reload`. These checks need a real character and protected-action execution; standalone mocks and the UI simulator cannot replace them. Record the client build, locale, character faction/class, QuickRoute commit, and relevant access state with the result. Do not describe an untested scenario as passed.

1. Open `/qr`, `/qrteleports`, `/qrmulti`, `/qr phases` and `/qr settings`. Check readable text, scrollable long lists, no overlapping controls, keyboard escape and screen fit at 75%, 100% and 150% scale. Check English and German.
2. Use a native map pin, Ctrl+Right-click and a TomTom point at distinct coordinates. Verify the final navigation pin remains at the chosen physical location, including a sub-map such as The Den. Select a different destination during an active vendor/trip comparison; stale results must not replace it.
3. Pick a dungeon entrance from the Journal and the addon picker. Follow the route to the actual entrance. Include Draenor, Midnight, an island requiring a transport, and a phase-dependent entrance. A missing connection must report no known route without inventing overland travel.
4. On a fresh character, verify locked portals, undiscovered taxis, unlearned spells and engineering toys without the profession are not offered as usable. Repeat after a relevant unlock. Do not use an all-unlocked test character as the only access test.
5. In a Zidormi region, verify routing in both phases and after an actual conversation. With unknown remote state, inspect the explicit assumption label. Selecting an assumption must not change the actual phase or silently complete a required step.
6. Follow one tracked quest objective, an unwatched logged quest, a completed quest's turn-in, and a known giver selected by ID. Verify live targets are used where provided, and catalogue references remain labeled. An absent objective must not become the giver's location or a map midpoint.
7. Select a currency and inspect its vendor list, then choose the fastest-known vendor. Verify the NPC sells an item for that currency. Visit a previously unknown merchant, close and reopen the picker, and check the new observation. Move continuously during comparison; work must finish, restart within its bound or ask for a stable position.
8. Paste a three-stop list and import TomTom points on different maps. Verify all stops remain present, the indicated teleport menu choice is usable, and completing a leg recalculates with consumed cooldowns. `/reload` midway: progress must persist for that character without auto-activating travel. Log into another character: their trip must be separate.
9. Bind at an inn, make a new camp where applicable, and visit an owned housing neighborhood. Check only observed coordinates are used. Move camp/rebind: the former location must disappear. Test a housing action chooses the intended owned house.
10. Test representative spell, toy and equipped-item buttons out of combat. Enter/leave combat with the route, map and quest tracker open. Protected controls must hide or remain safely configured; no taint, forbidden action or unintended secondary-click cast. Changing equipment manually must not be undone by stale restoration.
11. Use a random wormhole from the inventory. Confirm it is not represented by an invented exact landing in a deterministic route. For a selectable Mole Machine/wormhole, verify the named destination is actually unlocked and matches the chosen menu option.
12. Run `/qrtest graph`; inspect `/qrdiag` after the above actions. Record any errors together with the triggering action and the exact installed commit. Validate frame time during a 20-stop trip and a large vendor comparison on the actual client.

Follow-up cases from live feedback:

13. On a non-Worgen, check an account-owned Tess's Peacebloom. It must not be ready in either teleport view or offered by a map/route button. Compare an eligible Worgen and an unavailable toy API response. Cast a teleport with the inventory open: its availability must update when the cooldown starts and expires.
14. With and without ATT enabled, left-click and right-click a missing item in both grid and list views. Confirm the correct item opens, the explicit QuickRoute help remains accessible, and only a known source enables source routing. Switch to an owned item in a reused row: acquisition callbacks must not remain attached.
15. Change Settings categories, enter and clear a search, then close and reopen Settings. Branding must stay above QuickRoute's divider and disappear from other categories. Check Defaults and Route remain separate. At 150% preferred scale on a small screen, the main window's close button and bottom tabs must both remain visible.
16. Start a route, then remove its target or choose an unreachable target. Old travel buttons and QuickRoute navigation must disappear. Immediately replace the native map pin with your own: clearing the old route must preserve the replacement. Clear a route immediately after calculating it and confirm no delayed callback restores the arrow.
17. Open quest and currency search results, then change the quest objective or visit a merchant whose currency offer changed before clicking a result. The click must use current target/offer data or report that it is unavailable, rather than routing to the stale result.

Store results with the tested commit. Publishing this checklist is not evidence that the checks were run.
